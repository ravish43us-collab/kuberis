02 — Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Are there any hidden fees?

Never. The price you see is the price you pay — including e-filing and one state return. Additional states are a flat $25 each.

What if I need to upgrade mid-filing?

You can switch plans anytime and only pay the difference. Your documents and progress carry over instantly.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption, and we never sell your information. Ever.

Do I really get a human?

On Complete and Business, absolutely — a named, credentialed preparer who knows your file and is available year-round.
